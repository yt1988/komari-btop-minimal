# v1.6.17

## 中文

- 切走不足 10 秒且访问权限近期已确认时，切回立即显示原页面，并在后台取新快照。
- 首次进入、离开较久或上次权限核验已超过一分钟时，显示数据前重新核验。
- 切回时复用的实时连接两秒无响应即进入重连流程。

## English

- Instantly restore the previous view after a switch shorter than ten seconds if access was verified recently. Request fresh telemetry in the background.
- Recheck access before showing data on initial load, after a longer switch, or when access was last confirmed at least a minute ago.
- Reconnect promptly if a reused live connection remains unresponsive for two seconds after resuming.
