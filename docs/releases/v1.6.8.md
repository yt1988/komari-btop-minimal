# v1.6.8

## 中文

- 首页点击 CPU 百分比或上下行网速，直接进入该节点最近一小时的历史趋势。详情页可在 CPU、上下行网速和延迟之间切换，并沿用当前时间范围。
- 延迟图的光标按对应采样时段读数，分别显示实际延迟、无采样和 100% 丢包。
- 流量进度条前显示计量规则。↕ 表示上下行相加，↑ 和 ↓ 表示单向计量，MAX 和 MIN 分别表示取较大值和较小值。悬停、聚焦或点按可查看完整说明。

## English

- Select a CPU percentage or network speed in the node list to open that node’s last hour of history. Switch between CPU, network, and latency in the detail view with the same selected range.
- The latency chart reads the sample at the cursor and distinguishes measured latency, missing data, and 100% packet loss.
- Traffic quota bars show ↕ for combined traffic, ↑ or ↓ for one direction, and MAX or MIN for the higher or lower total. Hover, focus, or tap to read the full rule.
